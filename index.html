3.Indian Fruit & Vegetable Carts

UX Research & Product Design  |  Street Vendors

Improving Mobility, Safety & Usability for Street Vendors

I spoke with six street vendors and observed their daily work to understand the challenges they face while moving, handling, and using their carts.

Understand the physical and practical challenges vendors face while using their carts, and explore opportunities for improvement.
